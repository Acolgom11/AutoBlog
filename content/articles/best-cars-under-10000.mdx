## Introduction

Finding a reliable used car under $10,000 is getting harder in today's market, but it's not impossible. If you know exactly what models to look for and what common issues to avoid, you can still find an excellent daily driver.

These 5 models offer the best value for your money right now. We've compiled empirical data from thousands of ownership reports prioritizing:
- **Long-term reliability**
- **Maintenance costs**
- **Fuel efficiency**
- **Insurance premiums**

## 1. 2012-2015 Honda Civic
The definitive answer to "what cheap car should I buy?" The 9th generation Honda Civic remains an unstoppable force of reliability. With routine maintenance, the 1.8-liter engine easily sails past 200,000 miles.

### Pros
* Bulletproof engine and transmission
* Plentiful and cheap replacement parts
* Excellent fuel economy (30+ MPG combined)

### Cons
* Interior road noise at highway speeds
* Basic infotainment by modern standards

## 2. 2010-2013 Toyota Corolla
If excitement is at the very bottom of your priority list, but getting to work every single day without fail is at the top, the Corolla is your champion.

## 3. 2011-2014 Mazda 3
The Mazda 3 proves that driving a budget car doesn't have to feel like a punishment. It offers arguably the best driving dynamics in this price bracket.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are used cars under $10,000 reliable?
Yes, depending on the make and model. Prioritize renowned vehicles like Hondas and Toyotas with strong maintenance histories. 

### How many miles is too many?
It depends on the vehicle. A well-maintained Toyota with 150,000 miles is often a vastly superior choice to a neglected European luxury car with 80,000 miles.

## Conclusion
Patience is key when shopping in the $10,000 bracket. Always get a pre-purchase inspection from an independent mechanic before handing over your cash.
